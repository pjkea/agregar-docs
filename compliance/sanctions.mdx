## What happens

Every transaction is screened against sanctions, PEP and adverse-information sources. Screening runs on a dedicated service, not on this API, so there is no endpoint for you to call — it happens as part of ingestion.

| Signal | Source |
|---|---|
| **Sanctions** | OFAC, UN, EU and OpenSanctions |
| **PEP** | OpenSanctions politically-exposed-person records |
| **Adverse information** | OpenSanctions criminal, wanted and person-of-interest listings |

<Note>
  Adverse information means **structured listings** — criminal convictions, wanted notices, debarment — not press coverage. Someone written about in the media but not listed anywhere will not appear here. If your risk appetite requires news screening, that is a separate feed and worth raising with us.
</Note>

## How a hit affects the decision

A hit does **not** add points to the risk score. It **tightens the decision thresholds** for that transaction, which makes a `HOLD` or `BLOCK` far more likely at the same score:

| Hit | Effect |
|---|---|
| Sanctions | Strictest thresholds, typically `BLOCK`; a SANCTIONS case opens automatically |
| PEP | Stricter thresholds; an AML case opens automatically |
| Adverse information | Stricter thresholds |

See [Scoring and decisions](/concepts/scoring).

## A hit is not an HTTP error

Ingestion still returns `201 Created`. The request succeeded; the verdict comes back as **data**, and your core banking system is what enforces it.

Scoring is asynchronous by default, so the verdict is not in the ingestion response. Get it either way:

- poll `GET /api/v1/transactions/{transaction_id}` (needs a Bearer token), or
- subscribe to the `TRANSACTION_SCORED` [webhook](/guides/webhooks).

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "transaction_id": "TXN-X9Z3Q1",
  "combined_risk_score": 240,
  "flagged": true,
  "alert_id": "ALT-00123",
  "processing_complete": true
}
```

## Name matching

Matching is fuzzy, because sanctions lists and your customer records rarely agree on spelling, transliteration or name order. Scores band as:

| Score | Outcome |
|---|---|
| ≥ 95 | Match — treat as confirmed |
| 80-94 | Review — an analyst decides |
| < 80 | Clear |

Expect false positives in the review band. That is deliberate: in screening, a missed match costs far more than an extra review.

## Custom watchlists

You can maintain your own lists alongside the global ones — internal blocklists, previously-reported subjects, or names your compliance team wants flagged.

| Endpoint | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET /watchlists` | List your watchlists |
| `GET /watchlists/{name}/entries` | Entries in one list |
| `POST /watchlists/{name}/entries` | Add an entry |
| `DELETE /watchlists/{name}/entries/{value}` | Remove an entry |

These require a Bearer token.
